# Smart Cleaner Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Smart Cleaner (`com.mrzekai.depoakilli`) is provided by MrZekai. Privacy and support contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## Data accessed and processed on the device

Smart Cleaner analyzes accessible shared-storage file metadata. For exact duplicate detection, it may calculate content fingerprints locally. All files access is requested because storage management and file maintenance are core features: junk maintenance, exact duplicate detection, large-file review, Downloads/APK management, storage analysis, and WhatsApp shared-media management. Android continues to protect other apps' private internal data.

Usage Access is optional. When granted, it is used for Android-reported app storage/cache statistics and last-used information. Deep App Cache requests Android's official cache-cleaning action; Android controls the required confirmation.

Smart Cleaner does not upload personal files, file names, file paths, file contents, duplicate fingerprints, or installed-app lists to MrZekai.

## Third-party processing

Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform may process advertising identifiers, consent choices, diagnostics, and app interactions under Google's policies. Smart Cleaner does not include a third-party crash-reporting service. File names, paths, contents, duplicate fingerprints, installed-app lists, and user-entered text are not uploaded for crash reporting.

Data is transmitted to these providers using encrypted network connections. Their retention and deletion practices are governed by their published policies. Smart Cleaner does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

## File deletion and local retention

Files are removed only after user selection or an Android-controlled confirmation where Android requires one. Files you delete may not be recoverable by Smart Cleaner.

Preferences and aggregate scan/cleanup history are stored only in local app storage until you clear Smart Cleaner's app data or uninstall it. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes that local app data. It does not restore files that you previously chose to delete.

## Choices

You can deny or revoke All files access and Usage Access in Android Settings. Relevant storage-management features will then be limited or unavailable. Where required, the in-app advertising privacy control lets you review consent and personalization choices.

## Children

Smart Cleaner is not directed to children under 13. The Play Console target-audience declaration must remain consistent with this policy and the app's advertising configuration.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and its date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

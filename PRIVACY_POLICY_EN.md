# Smart Cleaner Privacy Policy

Smart Cleaner analyzes accessible shared-storage file metadata on the device. For exact duplicate detection it may calculate content fingerprints locally. Smart Cleaner does not upload personal files or cleaner-engine fingerprints.

All files access is requested because storage management and file maintenance are core features: junk maintenance, duplicate detection, large-file review, Downloads/APK management, storage analysis and WhatsApp shared-Media management. Android continues to protect other apps' private internal data.

Usage Access is optional and is used for app storage/cache statistics and last-used information. Deep App Cache requests Android's official cache-cleaning action; Android controls the required confirmation.

Google Mobile Ads and the consent platform may process advertising identifiers, diagnostics and consent choices under Google's policies.

Files are removed only after user selection or an Android-controlled confirmation where Android requires one.

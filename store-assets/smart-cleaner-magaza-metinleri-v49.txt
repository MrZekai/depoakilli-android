--- KISA ACIKLAMA ---
Clean up your phone: junk files, large videos, APKs, duplicates and app cache

--- TAM ACIKLAMA ---
Phone storage full? Smart Cleaner is a phone cleaner and storage manager for Android. Tap Smart Clean once and it scans your phone for junk files, large videos, old downloads, leftover APK installers and duplicate files, shows exactly what is taking up space, and lets you review everything before anything is deleted.

<b>No subscription. No account. Your files stay on your phone.</b>
Smart Cleaner is ad-supported, with no sign-up and no paid plan. Scanning and cleanup run on your device, and your files, file names and folder paths are never uploaded.

<b>Smart Clean: one scan, clear answers</b>

See how much space is safe to clean before you delete anything. Only low-risk items are preselected: junk files and verified extra copies of a file whose original is kept. Everything else waits for your choice.

Smart suggestions on the home screen point you to the biggest wins first: large files, leftover APKs and WhatsApp media.

<b>Cleanup tools</b>

Junk cleaner: find temporary files, old thumbnails and unfinished download leftovers.

Large file finder: find files over 100 MB of any type, including videos, archives and APKs.

Downloads and APK cleaner: review old downloads and installer files you no longer need. Removing an APK file does not uninstall the app.

Duplicate file finder: find exact copies of photos, videos and documents. Every duplicate is verified by its content, and one original is always kept.

Screenshot and media review: browse screenshots and large photos and videos in one place.

App cache cleaner and app manager: see how much cache each app uses, clear app cache with Android's official system tool, and review large or unused apps.

WhatsApp media cleaner: review images, videos, voice notes, documents and statuses from WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business, sorted by category.

<b>Storage tools</b>

Storage analyzer: see how much space photos, videos, audio, documents, archives and APKs use.

Storage change: compare two scans to see what grew or shrank on your storage.

<b>An honest cleaner: you stay in control</b>

No fake speed boosts and no scare warnings. Nothing is deleted without your confirmation. Scan results are suggestions, and you can open each category to check every file before cleaning. Deleted files cannot be restored, so review your selection carefully.

<b>Why the app asks for All files access</b>

Managing files is the core purpose of Smart Cleaner. To scan all folders, find duplicate files across file types and manage APKs, archives and documents, the app needs Android's All files access permission. The app explains why before asking, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings. Protected app folders such as Android/data and Android/obb are never accessed.

Usage access is optional. It is only used to show app storage and cache sizes and when apps were last used.

<b>Ads and consent</b>

Smart Cleaner is supported by ads from Google AdMob, and you can review your ad consent choices in the app. You can also watch a short optional video to hide ads for 60 minutes.

Available in English and Spanish, plus Portuguese (Brazil) and Turkish. Works on Android 11 and newer.

Clean up your phone and free up storage space safely, one reviewed file at a time.

Smart Cleaner is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Kullanım erişimi isteğe bağlıdır. Yalnızca uygulama depolama ve önbellek boyutlarını ve uygulamaların en son ne zaman kullanıldığını göstermek için kullanılır.

# Home design

Tabs: **Clean** (Home), **Tools**, **Trash**; Settings, About and privacy sit behind the gear icon.

## Clean tab, top to bottom
1. Storage line (used %, free space) and, while a quick scan runs, "Updating…" with Cancel.
2. Trash line when Trash holds files.
3. Recommendation card: "Up to X can be freed", "X selected safely", "X worth a look", and "Clean X" (selected bytes) or "Review X".
4. Included (Auto) rows, system steps (app cache, largest apps, unused apps), "Worth a look" rows with one labelled native ad after the 4th row.
5. Biggest folders, kept-files note, "Scan again".

Without all-files access the card is replaced by an explanation and "Allow access" / "Open settings". After a restart within 6 h, the saved totals are shown with "Show details".

There is no score ring, speed claim, Pro badge or paid tier. All numbers come from the scan.
